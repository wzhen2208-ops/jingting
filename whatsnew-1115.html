🔧 1. 科技背景（8）
Matt Palmer 与这期视频
Matt Palmer 在 SpaceX AI 工作（片尾自称 Matt with SpaceX AI），定期录《What's New in Grok Bot》介绍新功能。这期是特别版：Grok Bot 正式发布 1.0，视频说明里列了 8 条要点，Matt 口头归纳为五项。
1.0 和 beta
软件版本号 1.0 读 one point oh，通常表示第一个正式版。beta 是测试版，coming out of beta 就是“结束测试、转正”。Matt 说 1.0 代表产品更成熟、更稳定可靠。
Team Bots
据视频和说明：团队版、企业版用户可以一键把自己的 Bot 变成 Team Bot，不再复制一份；可以分享给个人、群组或全公司，也能放进 Slack；新的 Analytics 标签页显示省下的小时数和收到的消息数。
Bot 参加会议
据视频说明：对 Bot 说 “join my meeting at 11 AM”，它就会加入 Google Meet、Zoom 或 Microsoft Teams 会议，在会上听、在会议聊天里回复，并去 Slack、Notion 等连接器查你问的内容。
Jamie from JRE
JRE 是美国播客 The Joe Rogan Experience。节目里主持人常说 “Jamie, pull that up”，制作人 Jamie 就马上把资料调出来。Matt 用这个比喻说明：会议里的 Bot 是随叫随查的助手。
routine 与 BOM
routine 是 Grok Bot 的定时例行任务（比如每天早上 7 点自动运行）。BOM = bill of materials，物料清单。视频里的例子是：有产品发布时，Bot 早上自己去 Slack 找发布进展和 BOM，看有什么能主动做的。
Knowledge 设置
据视频：队友使用 Team Bot 时，Bot 会从中学习；Team Bots 里新增 Knowledge 设置，你可以批准、拒绝或编辑它学到的内容。Matt 把这比作“更新 Bot 的软件”。
PR、GitHub、Cursor 与 token
GitHub 是存放代码的平台；PR（pull request）是提交代码修改等人审核的申请；Cursor 是 AI 编程工具，cloud agents 是它在云端写代码的代理。token 是 AI 处理文字的计量单位，token efficiency 指用更少的 token 做同样的事。

🧩 3. 疑难句（14）
#2I'm in Dogpatch, San Francisco for another edition, a special edition of What's New in Grok Bot.
我在旧金山的 Dogpatch 街区，给大家带来又一期、而且是特别版的《Grok Bot 新功能》。
🧩 for another edition, a special edition of What's New in Grok Bot：Matt 先说 another edition，马上改口补充 a special edition（特别版）。字幕写成 a special of edition，音频经 whisper 复核是 a special edition of。
#9And we just want some really exciting features that make Grok Bot worth checking out if you haven't checked it out already.
我们还想推出一些真正让人兴奋的功能，让还没试过 Grok Bot 的人觉得值得一试。
🧩 we just want some really exciting features 在语法上不完整（音频确实是 want），大意是“我们想推出一些很棒的功能”；that make Grok Bot worth checking out 修饰 features；if you haven't checked it out already 是给新用户说的。
#10And if you're already using the product, it makes Grok Bot something to watch from a consistency, a reliability standpoint.
如果你已经在用，那从稳定性、可靠性的角度来看，Grok Bot 也值得关注。
🧩 it makes Grok Bot something to watch：让它成为值得关注的产品；from a consistency, a reliability standpoint 是两个名词共用一个 standpoint，Matt 说出 consistency 后又换成 reliability。
#13Now, if you've heard me talk about Team Bots before, if you're a Teams or Enterprise customer, you can basically take any bot you've created and create what we call a Team Bot that you can then put in Slack, you can share with your coworkers in their Grok Bot instances.
如果你之前听我讲过 Team Bots：如果你是团队版或企业版客户，基本上可以把你建的任何一个 Bot 做成我们所说的 Team Bot，然后放进 Slack，或者分享给同事，在他们各自的 Grok Bot 里用。
🧩 长句骨架：if ..., if ..., you can basically take any bot ... and create what we call a Team Bot。后面的 that you can then put in Slack, you can share with your coworkers ... 都是说 Team Bot 能干什么。
#20Now there's a bit of a analytics tab where you can see the number of hours you've saved, the number of messages that have been sent to that Team Bot,
现在有一个分析标签页，可以看到它帮你省了多少小时，以及有多少条消息发给了这个 Team Bot；
🧩 a bit of a analytics tab：口误，按语法应为 an analytics tab；where you can see 后面两个 the number of ... 并列，说明标签页里能看到什么。
#31that can actually be in the chat if you're in a Google Meet, if you're in a Zoom, and can both respond to chats, but then also verbal cues, verbal instructions.
如果你用 Google Meet 或 Zoom 开会，它还能出现在聊天区里：既能回复聊天消息，也能回应口头提示和口头指令。
🧩 这句接第 30 句：that can actually be in the chat 的 that 指 AI teammate；if you're in a Google Meet, if you're in a Zoom 是两个条件；can both respond to chats, but then also verbal cues 是口语版的 both A and B。

🧩 You know, maybe my, I call my bot Spin that's in these meetings：说到 maybe my 断掉，重新开头 I call my bot Spin（我把参会的 Bot 叫作 Spin）；that's in these meetings 修饰 my bot。
#42So all that stuff, you know, when you're in a meeting and you're like, oh, we'll circle back to that.
开会时经常有这种情况：你会说“哦，这个我们回头再说”。
🧩 So all that stuff 开了个头没说完，接着换成 when you're in a meeting and you're like, oh, we'll circle back to that。you're like = 你会说。
#56Your bot in the morning, maybe the 7 AM routine is going to go out and say, what can I do to help Matt with this launch?
早上，你的 Bot（也许是早上 7 点的例行任务）就会出动，想一想：我能帮 Matt 为这次发布做点什么？
🧩 Your bot in the morning, maybe the 7 AM routine 是两次说主语（第二次更具体），真正的谓语是 is going to go out and say；引号里是 Bot 的“内心独白”。
#57So your bot might go through Slack and try to find, for example, the status of the launch, try to find the BOM, the bill of materials, try to find if there are things it can do proactively to help you get started.
你的 Bot 可能会翻一遍 Slack，试着找到比如发布的进展、BOM（物料清单），看看有没有它能主动做的事，帮你把工作启动起来。
🧩 三个 try to find 并列：the status of the launch / the BOM, the bill of materials / if there are things it can do proactively；最后 to help you get started 说明目的。
#59We're trying to make those teammates have agency and take proactivity to helping you do things that maybe you would have missed otherwise.
我们想让这些队友有自主性、主动出手，帮你把那些原本可能会漏掉的事情做好。
🧩 make those teammates have agency and take proactivity：make + 宾语 + 动词原形；take proactivity to helping you 是口语拼凑（等于 proactively help you）；that maybe you would have missed otherwise 修饰 things。
#65Now, in the past, if you're building software for someone, that meant that you had to go in and change that software, you had to update that software so that it was more useful or so that it could do new things.
过去，如果你为别人开发软件，就得自己进去改软件、更新软件，才能让它更好用、或者能做新的事情。
🧩 in the past, if you're building software for someone, that meant that you had to ...：过去的情况；后面两个 you had to 并列，两个 so that 并列说明目的。
