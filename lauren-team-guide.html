04 / 08

• tell your bots to create skills for itself as you give it instructions on how to do tasks. this lets them do them more consistently even over long conversations. you can even ask it to create a private github repo for you to store all your skills if you want them preserved independently

中文意思

• 你教 Bot 某件事怎么做的时候，让它顺手把做法存成自己的“技能”。这样即使对话拉得很长，它做事也能保持一致。想让这些技能独立保存的话，还可以让它帮你建一个私有的 GitHub 仓库，把所有技能都放进去。

④ 工具

• add connectors, use the vault, and give access to all the tools you use to do your work. you can also ask your bot to setup its own email and it can sign up for its own accounts for better separation

中文意思

• 加上连接器、用好保险库（vault），把你工作中用到的工具都开放给它。你还可以让 Bot 给自己开一个邮箱，用它注册自己的账号，这样它的账号和你的分开，更清楚。

  • sign up for注册（账号、服务）
  • separation分开，隔离
⑤ 找手工活

中文意思

⑥ 当实习生带
07 / 08

• imagine you hired an intern, how would you onboard them and help them do the work correctly and efficiently? introduce yourself to your bot and describe the work you do, then ask it how it can help you automate it

中文意思

• 假设你招了个实习生，你会怎么带他入职，帮他把活干对、干快？对 Bot 也一样：先介绍你自己、讲清楚你做什么工作，然后问它可以怎么帮你把这些工作自动化。

本段生词 4
  • hire an intern招一个实习生
  • onboard带新人入职，让新人上手
  • correctly and efficiently又对又快

⭐ 最爱技巧
08 / 08

and my favorite tip: use voice mode or the transcription option! it’s a good way to just brain dump a lot of information. then, at the end, tell your bot to “restate this in your own words” so you can confirm it understood you

中文意思

还有我最喜欢的一个技巧：用语音模式或者语音转文字！这样可以一口气把脑子里的一大堆信息全倒出来。最后再对 Bot 说一句“用你自己的话复述一遍”，就能确认它真的听懂了你的意思。

本段生词 5

  • brain dump把脑子里的想法一股脑倒出来
  • restate … in your own words用你自己的话复述
  • confirm确认

04疑难句

6 句 · 拆开就明白
句子 01

tell your bots to create skills for itself as you give it instructions on how to do tasks.

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. tell your bots to create skills —— 主干：tell sb to do sth，让 Bot 去建技能
  2. for itself —— 给它自己用（主语是复数 bots，按语法应是 themselves；随手写的帖子，单复数混用）
  3. as you give it instructions —— as = 在……的同时，你边教它
  4. on how to do tasks —— instructions on how to…：关于怎么做事的说明

你一边教 Bot 怎么做事，一边让它把做法存成自己的技能。

↑ 第4段
句子 02

you can even ask it to create a private github repo for you to store all your skills if you want them preserved independently

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. ask it to create a private github repo —— 让它建一个私有的 GitHub 仓库
  2. for you to store all your skills —— for sb to do：给你用来存放所有技能
  3. if you want them preserved independently —— want + 宾语 + 过去分词：想让它们被独立保存

想让技能独立保存的话，甚至可以让它给你建个私有 GitHub 仓库来放。

↑ 第4段
句子 03

you can also ask your bot to setup its own email and it can sign up for its own accounts for better separation

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. ask your bot to setup its own email —— 让 Bot 开自己的邮箱（setup 规范写作 set up）
  2. and it can sign up for its own accounts —— 再用这个邮箱注册它自己的账号
  3. for better separation —— 目的：和你的账号分得更开

让 Bot 开自己的邮箱、注册自己的账号，和你的分开。

↑ 第5段
句子 04

thinking about all the work you do by hand, or steps in your workflows where you have to copy and paste information back to your bot.

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. thinking about … —— 想一想……（这里用 -ing 开头当祈使句，规范写法是 think about）
  2. all the work you do by hand —— 你手工做的所有活（you do by hand 是省略 that 的定语从句）
  3. or steps in your workflows —— 或者流程里的那些步骤
  4. where you have to copy and paste information back to your bot —— where 引导定语从句修饰 steps：在这些步骤里你得把信息复制粘贴回去给 Bot

想想哪些活还靠手工，哪些步骤要你把信息复制粘贴给 Bot。

↑ 第6段
句子 05

imagine you hired an intern, how would you onboard them and help them do the work correctly and efficiently?

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. imagine you hired an intern —— 假设你招了个实习生（hired 用过去式表示假设）
  2. how would you onboard them —— would 表示假设情况下“你会怎么做”；them 指性别不明的单个人
  3. and help them do the work —— help sb do sth，帮他干活
  4. correctly and efficiently —— 又对又快

假如你招了个实习生，你会怎么带他、帮他把活干得又对又快？

↑ 第7段
句子 06

then, at the end, tell your bot to “restate this in your own words” so you can confirm it understood you

句子拆解与中文意思
  1. then, at the end —— 然后在最后
  2. tell your bot to “restate this in your own words” —— 让 Bot 用自己的话复述
  3. so you can confirm —— so = 这样就能……（表目的）
  4. it understood you —— 它理解了你（understood 是过去式，指刚才那番话）

最后让 Bot 用自己的话复述一遍，就能确认它听懂了。
