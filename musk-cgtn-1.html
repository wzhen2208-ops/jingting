🔧 1. 科技背景（9）
CGTN / CMG
CGTN 是中国国际电视台，隶属中央广播电视总台（China Media Group, CMG）。本片是总台对马斯克的独家专访。
Tesla Gigafactory Shanghai
特斯拉上海超级工厂，2019 年投产，是特斯拉全球产量最大的工厂之一。
Cybercab
特斯拉无人驾驶出租车，没有方向盘、踏板和后视镜，靠 FSD 自动驾驶运行。
xAI · Grok · Grok Bot
xAI 是马斯克的 AI 公司；Grok 是它的大模型（本片说到 Grok 4.7）；Grok Bot 是基于 Grok 的个人 AI 助手。
Anthropic · Claude Opus
Anthropic 是另一家前沿 AI 公司，Claude Opus 是它的旗舰模型，以写代码强著称。
frontier 前沿
AI 圈说 frontier model / catch up to frontier，指能力最强的那一档模型。
compute 算力 · lithography 光刻
算力就是训练和运行 AI 需要的芯片计算资源；光刻是造芯片最关键的工艺，高端光刻机是中国芯片的主要瓶颈。
per capita 人均
按人口平均。马斯克的算法：中国人口约是美国 4 倍，人均用电追平美国，总发电量就约是美国 4 倍。
Optimus
特斯拉的人形机器人。actuator（执行器）是让关节动起来的电机部件，sensor 是传感器。

🧩 3. 疑难句（11）
#10And it's also obvious to see, just walking around the streets, the increased prosperity of the average Chinese citizen has increased tremendously.
而且走在街上就明显能看出，普通中国百姓富裕了很多。
🧩 主干 it's obvious to see (that) the increased prosperity … has increased tremendously；中间插入 just walking around the streets（走在街上就能看出）。口语里 increased 说了两遍。
#15So what kind of advantages or breakthroughs do you think made the Shanghai factory a very important position in Tesla's global blueprint?
那你觉得是什么优势或突破，让上海工厂在特斯拉全球布局中占据这么重要的位置？
🧩 记者非母语句式：what advantages … do you think made the Shanghai factory a very important position in Tesla's global blueprint? ＝ 是什么让上海工厂在全球布局中这么重要。
#21To be totally… Because it's in China, at least, right?
说实话……因为它在中国，至少是这样，对吧？
🧩 两人同时说话：Musk 说 to be totally…，记者插 because of Chinese，Musk 接 because it's in China, at least, right?
#38Well, we have the Cybercab, which, as you know, has no steering wheel or pedals or side mirrors or rearview mirror, operating commercially in Texas, right?
嗯，Cybercab 你知道，没有方向盘、踏板、侧后视镜和车内后视镜，已经在得州商业运营了。
🧩 which 引出一长串 no steering wheel or pedals or side mirrors or rearview mirror，主干是 we have the Cybercab … operating commercially in Texas。
#47You know, sometimes when I go to bed, there'll be some big AI breakthrough, and then when I wake up, there's an AI breakthrough, and by the time it gets to lunch, there's another AI breakthrough.
有时候我睡前出了个 AI 大突破，醒来又一个，到午饭时又来一个。
🧩 三段时间：when I go to bed / when I wake up / by the time it gets to lunch，每段都有一个 AI breakthrough。
#55And it's a cool… Grok 4.7 is a solid workhorse of a model.
Grok 4.7 是个扎实耐用的“老黄牛”型模型。
🧩 a solid workhorse of a model ＝ a model that is a solid workhorse。a … of a … 这个结构母语者很常用。
#64because Grok 4.7 is already being trained on SpaceX real-world engineering data.
因为 Grok 4.7 已经在用 SpaceX 的真实工程数据训练了。
🧩 字幕写 grok 1 7 … trained by uh spacex，本页按语境整理为 Grok 4.7 … trained on SpaceX real-world engineering data；下一句 Musk 马上纠正 only a little bit so far。
#71But no one has yet made AI excellent at real-world engineering, and I think that's an opportunity for Grok, using the training data from SpaceX and Tesla, to be excellent at real-world engineering.
但还没人让 AI 精通真实工程，我觉得这是 Grok 的机会，用 SpaceX 和特斯拉的数据把它做好。
🧩 no one has yet made AI excellent at … ＝ 还没人做到；后半句 that's an opportunity for Grok, using the training data …, to be excellent at …，for Grok … to be 被插入语隔开。
#78And as I've said publicly, you know, I think China's going to address the compute limitation, I think faster than most people expect.
我公开说过，我认为中国会比大多数人预期的更快解决算力限制问题。
🧩 I think 说了两遍：I think China's going to address the compute limitation, I think faster than most people expect。
#89So if China reaches the same per capita electricity output as the US, which I think is likely, then China would go from where it is now, roughly three times the US electricity production, to probably four times,
所以如果中国人均发电量达到美国的水平——我认为很可能——那中国就会从现在大约美国三倍的发电量，涨到大约四倍，
🧩 长句：if China reaches …（which I think is likely）, then China would go from where it is now (roughly three times …) to probably four times。字幕这里不清楚（写成 the same gdp），本页按语境整理为 per capita。
#104But a robot, you can just improve the actuators, improve the sensors, improve the frame rate of the cameras, increase the amount of AI compute,
但机器人你可以直接升级执行器、传感器、摄像头帧率，增加 AI 算力，
🧩 一串并列：improve the actuators, improve the sensors, improve the frame rate of the cameras, increase the amount of AI compute；语速很快。
