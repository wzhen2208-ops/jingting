🔧 1. 科技背景（9）
DeepMind / Google DeepMind
2010 年 Hassabis 在伦敦联合创办的 AI 研究公司，2014 年被 Google 收购，现在是 Google 的 AI 核心部门，做 Gemini 等模型。
AlphaFold
DeepMind 开发的 AI，能从氨基酸序列预测蛋白质三维结构，解决了生物学 50 年难题；Hassabis 因此获 2024 年诺贝尔化学奖。
Isomorphic Labs
Hassabis 创办的 AI 药物研发公司，用 AlphaFold 这类技术设计新药。
AGI 通用人工智能
在几乎所有认知任务上都达到人类水平的 AI。Hassabis 的使命就是“安全地”把 AGI 带到世上。
深度学习 + 强化学习
深度学习：用多层神经网络从大量数据学规律；强化学习：通过试错和奖励来学会做决策（AlphaGo 就是这样学下围棋）。
compute 算力
AI 圈把 compute 当名词用，指训练和运行模型需要的计算资源（GPU 等）。
due diligence 尽职调查
收购或投资前，买方对公司技术、财务、法律等做全面审查。
《主题公园》Theme Park
1994 年的经营模拟游戏，Hassabis 17 岁参与设计和编程。玩家建游乐园，游客有自己的行为。
AI 智能体安全
AI agent 能自己上网、写代码、执行操作；如果被滥用，可能用于网络攻击。Hassabis 主张设立类似标准机构，对前沿 AI 测试和认证。

🧩 3. 疑难句（10）
#7Chess prodigy at 12 years old, designed and programmed the '90s video game "Theme Park," and, of course, co-founded DeepMind,
12 岁就是国际象棋神童，设计并编写了 90 年代的电子游戏《主题公园》，当然还联合创办了 DeepMind，
🧩 长串履历没有主语：(You were a) chess prodigy…, (you) designed…, co-founded…；听的时候抓三个名词：chess、Theme Park、DeepMind。
#24So I thought at least if it didn't work, as we were thinking about setting up DeepMind, it would not work in an interesting new way.
所以在我们筹划创办 DeepMind 时我想，就算失败，至少也会以一种有意思的新方式失败。
🧩 主干 I thought (that) at least if it didn't work, it would not work in an interesting new way；中间插入 as we were thinking about setting up DeepMind（“当时我们正筹划创办 DeepMind”）。意思：就算失败也要失败得有价值。
#41I guess if I was to think what's the most consequential, it's probably starting DeepMind in 2010, where pretty much nobody thought AI would work.
如果要说影响最大的，大概是 2010 年创办 DeepMind，那时几乎没人相信 AI 能成。
🧩 if I was to think … = 要我说……；“where pretty much nobody thought AI would work”的 where 指 2010 年那个时候。
#57but certainly orders of magnitude or two orders of magnitude more than we were able to access at the time as a small startup.
但肯定比我们当时作为小创业公司能拿到的多出一两个数量级。
🧩 orders of magnitude or two orders of magnitude：先说“几个数量级”再改口“两个数量级”；more than we were able to access … as a small startup。
#62because we thought we were onto something really big, but we didn't want the other big companies necessarily to catch on yet while we were still doing the research.
因为我们觉得自己摸到了很大的东西，但还在做研究时，不想让其他大公司察觉。
🧩 because 引出两层：we thought we were onto something big（摸到大东西），but we didn't want the other big companies … to catch on yet（不想让大公司察觉）；necessarily 插在中间，语速快容易漏。
#64because once you had some of their top researchers and programmers, I remember Jeff Dean at the time looking through our code,
因为一旦他们的顶尖研究员和程序员（我记得当时 Jeff Dean 就在翻看我们的代码），
🧩 once you had some of their top researchers… 说到一半插入 I remember Jeff Dean at the time looking through our code，主句在下一句 they've now seen all the secrets。
#79This is a critical thing to get right, and it's why I've been sort of calling for things like potentially some kind of standards body to test and certify frontier AI systems.
这件事必须做对，所以我一直在呼吁成立类似标准机构的组织，来测试和认证前沿 AI 系统。
🧩 it's why I've been sort of calling for things like potentially some kind of standards body：口语里 sort of / things like / potentially / some kind of 一堆缓冲词，核心是 calling for a standards body to test and certify frontier AI。
#93And I think there could be, obviously, the reason I've worked my whole life on this technology is I think it can enable an incredible new future,
当然，我一辈子都在做这项技术，原因是我相信它能开启一个不可思议的新未来，
🧩 And I think there could be, obviously, — 说一半放弃，重新开头 the reason I've worked my whole life on this technology is I think it can enable an incredible new future。
#109and then you can look at all, collect the statistics of what happened and then back that up to the current decision and then make a better decision.
然后统计每种结果，再倒推回当前的决策，做出更好的决定。
🧩 back that up to the current decision：不是“备份”，是把统计结果“倒推回”当前的决策。
#119You just suddenly get a call, and it's got a Swedish number on it, and you're vaguely aware in the background that that's the time of year for Nobels.
突然接到个电话，显示是瑞典号码，你心里隐约知道这正是诺贝尔奖公布的时节。
🧩 you're vaguely aware in the background that that's the time of year for Nobels：两个 that 连读 “that that's”，第一个是连词，第二个是代词。
